Sales Commission Policy — Membership Sales Consultant

STRONG Pilates Bankstown, Castle Hill, Leppington, Wetherill Park and Penrith — Effective 20 October 2026

1. About this policy

This policy sets out how sales commission is earned, credited and paid for the Membership Sales Consultant role at STRONG Pilates Bankstown, Castle Hill, Leppington, Wetherill Park and Penrith. It covers sales for the studios you are assigned to in your employment agreement. It is the commission policy referred to in your employment agreement. Your hourly rate and commission amounts are set in that agreement; this policy explains how sales are credited and paid.

The Sales Director reviews and approves all commission each month. Where this policy needs interpreting, the Sales Director decides, and that decision is final.

Trainers and front-of-house staff can also earn commission on sales they process. This does not reduce commission you have earned under this policy.

2. Rates and what counts as a sale

Sale typeCommission (inclusive of super)
New recurring membership — sales 1 to 10 in the month, per studio$25 per sale
New recurring membership — sale 11 onwards in the month, per studio$50 per sale
Class pack or sauna pack (new, renewal or repeat purchase)10% of the price the customer pays

The 10-sale threshold is counted per studio, per calendar month, on memberships credited to you. Class packs and sauna packs do not count towards it.

A new recurring membership includes a membership taken out by a former member whose previous membership had ended. It does not include a change of plan, a membership returning from suspension, or a cancellation reversed within 30 days.

No commission is paid on intro offers, casual classes or any other transaction, unless agreed in writing on a case-by-case basis.

3. When a sale is yours

A sale is credited to you in either of these cases.

You processed the sale yourself. Either:

  • you set up the membership or pack in the system and the first payment was charged to the customer's nominated card or account; or

  • the customer purchased online or in the app using a code assigned to you, whether or not you were on the phone with them at the time.

If you guided a customer through an app purchase and no code of yours was used, the sale is treated as processed by someone else, and your notes decide under the next heading.

Someone else processed the sale (in studio, online or otherwise), and your CRM notes show that within the 30 days before the sale, at least one of the following happened:

  • The prospect indicated to you that they intended to sign up.

  • You recommended a specific membership or class pack and discussed its price.

  • You had a genuine membership conversation with the prospect that went beyond standard follow-up (for example, they asked about pricing or options, or raised an objection you handled), and your notes show the conversation continued until the sale, or you set a follow-up task for it.

  • You identified the prospect to the studio team as ready to be asked for the membership sale, with the details they needed (where the prospect is at, what they are leaning towards).

Conditions that apply to all of the above:

  • The notes must have been recorded in the CRM before the sale, not added afterwards.

  • The contact must have been two-way: a call the prospect answered, or a message they replied to.

  • Where the tracker and the CRM differ, the CRM and payment records decide.

The following do not qualify on their own:

  • Booking a prospect into a trial or class.

  • Standard follow-up without a genuine membership conversation.

  • Unanswered calls or text messages, however many.

  • Simply entering a lead into the CRM.

If none of the above applies, the sale is not yours, regardless of how many times you tried to reach the prospect.

4. Payment

  • Commission is calculated monthly, per calendar month.

  • A sale counts in the month the customer's first payment is received, not the month it was sold. A membership sold on 28 March with its first payment received on 2 April counts in April. A pending membership, where the first payment has not yet been charged or has failed, is not a closed sale and earns no commission until the payment comes through.

  • You must record each sale in the sales tracker provided to you. Commission is paid on sales that appear in both the tracker and the CRM.

  • After month end, the Sales Director reviews and approves commission, then submits it to payroll. Commission is paid 2 to 3 weeks after the end of the month it relates to.

  • Commission on sales whose first payment is received before your employment ends is paid in the normal cycle.

5. Cancellations and refunds

  • A sale that is cancelled or refunded before commission for that month is approved is not paid.

  • If commission has been paid and the customer's payment is later refunded, reversed or not received, the commission is repayable.

  • For memberships, this applies only where the member does not complete 4 weeks of paid membership. A cancellation after 4 weeks does not affect your commission.

  • For class packs, any refund of the pack means the commission on it is repayable.

  • Repayable commission is deducted from your next commission payment. The Sales Director will set out the sales and amounts in writing before any deduction is made.

6. Disputes

  • Each month you will receive a list of the sales credited to you for that month.

  • If you believe a sale is missing or wrongly credited, raise it with the Sales Director in writing within 7 days of receiving the list. After that, the month is closed.

  • The Sales Director will decide from the CRM notes and payment records, normally within 7 days, and that decision is final.

  • The same 7-day rule applies to trainers and front-of-house staff under their policy, so disputes about the same sale are decided together.

7. Changes to this policy

The business may update this policy from time to time with reasonable written notice. Changes apply to sales made after the change takes effect. Commission already earned under the previous version is not affected.
