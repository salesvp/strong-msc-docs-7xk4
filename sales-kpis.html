Sales KPIs — Membership Sales Consultant

STRONG Pilates Castle Hill and Bankstown — Effective 20 October 2026

1. About these KPIs

These are the key performance indicators for the Membership Sales Consultant role. They are the measures referred to in clause 5 of your employment agreement and are how performance in the role, including during probation, is assessed.

KPIs are measured monthly and reviewed with you each month. The KPIs below apply from the date shown until they are replaced. They may change to reflect seasonality, campaigns, lead volume and business priorities. Any change will be given to you in writing with a new from date, before it applies.

Results are measured from the CRM, the sales tracker and the monthly commission approval.

Your commission is set by the Sales Commission Policy and is not changed by this document.

4. How KPIs are reviewed

  • Weekly, in the sales meeting: activity for the week, from the CRM, and the pipeline for the week ahead.

  • Monthly: results against minimum and target, taken from the monthly commission approval. This is also when next month's KPIs are confirmed.

  • Probation is assessed on monthly results and consistent pipeline work.
